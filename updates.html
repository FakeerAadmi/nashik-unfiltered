Kondaji Chivda Heritage Lore & Pricing Verified

Sayantara Sabudana Vada Fasting Timings Confirmed

Sayantara operates 8:30 AM to 9:30 PM daily in Bhadrakali. Prices adjusted: Sabudana Vada plate now ₹60 (two large vadas with fresh cucumber-crushed chilli chutney).

Vande Bharat CSMT–Nashik Road Schedule Verified

Train #22223 departs Mumbai CSMT at 06:20 AM and arrives at Nashik Road at 09:55 AM (3 hrs 35 mins via Kasara Ghat banking engines). Chair car fare ₹860 verified.

Harihar Fort Forest Department Checkpost Update

Maharashtra Forest Department now enforces a ₹50 per person conservation entry fee at Nirgudpada base village. Weekend group limits capped at 1,500 trekkers to prevent crowding on the 80° rock chimney.

JULY 10, 2026

Sula Tasting Room Rates & Booking Advisory

Weekend walk-in tasting tours frequently sell out by 2:00 PM. Reserve 48 hours in advance online. Premium tasting flight with 6 wines is ₹600 per adult.
