CIVIC RECORD OCTOBER 2026 DISPATCH

Simhastha Kumbh Begins October 31, 2026: The ₹35,000 Crore Transformation

The Maharashtra State Cabinet has officially sanctioned an all-inclusive ₹35,000 crore development blueprint for the Simhastha Kumbh Mela. Ceremonial Dhwajarohan (sacred flag-hoisting) will take place at Ramkund and Trimbakeshwar on October 31, 2026, commencing a 21-month sacred calendar through July 24, 2028.

Unlike previous Melas where temporary works were dismantled post-event, this blueprint invests entirely in permanent municipal assets: a 66-kilometer Outer Ring Road, 21 new bridges spanning the Godavari, Darna, and Nandini rivers, and vast riverfront retention bunds designed to prevent historical monsoon flooding.

For visitors traveling between now and 2028, expect localized road diversions around Panchavati and Dwarka circle, but dramatically improved highway transit arriving from Mumbai and Pune.

Nashik Ozar Airport (ISK) Getting ₹556 Crore Upgrades

The new civil passenger terminal at HAL Ozar is undergoing rapid expansion. Direct daily flights to Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Ahmedabad are being stabilized, with an extended apron capacity for 8 narrowbody aircraft.

6-Lane Nashik–Trimbakeshwar Corridor Underway

The notoriously congested 28 km two-lane pilgrimage road connecting Nashik city to the Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga is being widened to a divided 6-lane expressway with dedicated pedestrian pilgrim tracks.
